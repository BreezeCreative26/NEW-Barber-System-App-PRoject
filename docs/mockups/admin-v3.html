A clickable mock-up of what the shop sees. It's a preview only. Use the buttons below to play out what happens.

Booking in progress

  • When an online customer taps a time, a striped green block appears in that barber's column, with a countdown.
  • It turns amber in its last minute. Customers can be held for 5 minutes at most, or 2 minutes after leaving the page.
  • If they confirm, it becomes a normal appointment and the bell shows New booking. If not, it fades away.
  • Click the block to see who it is (if signed in), or book over it for a walk-in. You'll be warned first, and the customer is offered nearby times.

Notifications (bell)

  • × to clear one item (with Undo), plus Clear all.
  • Mark all read button. Opening the bell no longer marks everything read by itself.
  • Grouped by Today / Yesterday / Earlier, with how long ago.
  • Activity and Needs attention tabs. The red number only counts unread activity.
  • "Needs attention" items can be fixed or dismissed.
